## Status

_Last updated 2026-10-19._

**Just shipped:** `/triangle` (c222eb2), live at
https://higher-ed-database.vercel.app/triangle. A student picks three four-year
colleges, and the page shows what is unique to each, shared by each pair, and
common to all three. It covers traits (type, setting, region, size, highest
degree, selectivity, SAT, graduation rate, cost, HBCU and similar flags) and
bachelor's majors at the 4-digit CIP level. There is a "Compare 3" link in the
nav.
- Logic: `src/lib/triangle.ts`. The trait bands live in `FACETS`.
- Queries: `getTriangleProfiles` and `searchUndergradInstitutions` in
  `src/db/queries.ts`.
- UI: `src/app/triangle/`.
- Autocomplete API: `src/app/api/institutions/search`.
- Major names come from `src/lib/cip-series-labels.ts`, which is generated by
  `scripts/gen-cip-labels.mjs` from an IPEDS .accdb. Rerun the script if CIP
  codes change.
- The page queries live, without precomputation (three indexed lookups).
